# Contract: What the member is shown and told

Source of truth is the code named beside each group; this list is for review and for the tour and stake sheet in `specs/001-holdout-hackathon` (T042, T059), which must reuse the same sentences.

## The staked rule — one sentence, everywhere (FR-012, FR-014)

`Stakes.STAKED_RULE`:

> Staked Holdouts are counted at 45°: stand the phone on the floor ahead of your shoulder, turned to look at you at about 45° — not in front, not straight beside you — and do full push-ups from your toes. Push-ups from the knees do not count.

Shown today in the set-up guide for staked sittings. Must appear on the stake sheet (001 T059) and the tour's counting card (001 T042).

## Refused placement (FR-004) — `Stakes.cameraFix`

- Daily 25: "Put the phone in front of you, or at 45°"
- Staked: "Turn the phone to 45°"

## Getting into position (FR-018, FR-019) — `Coach`, in priority order, shown large and spoken

More light · Stand phone up · Get in view · Phone to 45° · Get down · Further · Closer · Move left · Move right · Hold

A moving background is not a correction: its pill turns amber with "Find a spot with nothing moving behind you", and the countdown still starts.

## Refused reps — `RepHint`, shown and spoken

- "Go lower" — too shallow
- "Keep your body straight" — folded, or cobra
- "Slow down" — too fast
- "Knees off the floor" — staked only
- "Keep your knees in the picture" — staked only: the near knee unseen, or at the picture's edge, on the push; said aloud the first time

## Framing (FR-024, FR-010) — the whole-body check, before the hold

- "Keep your head in the picture" — the head at or past an edge
- "Keep your knees in the picture — move the phone further away" — staked: the near knee at or past an edge
- "Turn your body slightly to your left (right), away from the phone, so it sees your knees" — staked: the near knee hidden behind the body; the coach says "Turn slightly left" or "Turn slightly right" by the side the phone is on ("Turn slightly away" when that is unclear)
- While counting nothing is said about the head *(revised 2026-09-29)*

## Feedback (FR-021)

- A short tone and a rising "+1" per counted rep; the banked chime instead on every tenth.
