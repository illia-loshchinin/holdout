## 1. What this is

A fitness commitment contract. A member stakes on themselves to complete a daily push-up obligation for the length of a fixed cohort. Everyone in a cohort starts and finishes together. Miss a day and the stake is forfeited into that cohort's prize pool; finish and take back the stake plus a share of what the quitters left behind.

> Stake. Do your reps every day.
> Miss a day and you lose your stake to everyone still standing.
> Finish and you get your stake back plus a share of theirs.

**Why it works as a product:** the cohort is visible and it shrinks. `189 / 312 still standing` is a number no rolling-join competitor can display, and it is the reason to open the app on a day when nothing else happened.

**Why it is being built now:** a submission to CLOCK IN, the Solana Mobile hackathon, judged from 13 October to 9 November 2026 in four equal parts — stickiness and product fit with the Seeker community (habits and daily engagement), user experience, innovation, and presentation and demo quality — not a startup launch.

## 1a. What members call things

| Members read | The system stores | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **The Daily 25** | no record on-chain; a device-local streak | The free tier: 25 push-ups, any day, alone, nothing staked (FR-048) |
| **A Holdout** — "a 7-day Holdout", "a 21-day Holdout" | `Cohort`, in one of the two leagues | A staked run. "189 still standing in this Holdout" |
| **Spark · 7-day** and **Habit · 21-day** | the league's `form`: 1 (`Easy`, knees allowed) and 0 (`Hard`, from the toes) | The two levels of a Holdout, always named level first, then length. Never "Easy", "Hard", "staked", "league" or "cohort" to a member (2026-10-11, constitution XIII; FR-021a). A short test cohort keeps its own label (FR-044a) |
| **Today · Standing · Holdouts · Me** | the four tabs of the bottom bar | "Home" in this spec means **Today**, the first tab (FR-069) |
| **Still standing** | memberships not in breach | Never "survivors left alive" or similar |
| **At stake** | the sum of held stakes in a cohort | What a lobby shows. It is **not** a prize |
| **Prize pool** | `Cohort.pool` | Only a running Holdout has one, because only forfeits create it (FR-041) |

On-chain and technical names — `Cohort`, `LeagueConfig`, cohort index — are unchanged, so the data model, contracts and automation keep their vocabulary. This table is the mapping between the two.

- Q: How does a judge see a full cycle (join → sessions → forfeit → settle → claim) without waiting days, while the home screen always shows a live, thinning cohort? - Q: Should audio be dropped from rep verification entirely — both the environment check and the rep condition? (OD-3) → A: Yes — drop both. Four environment checks, no audio rep condition, no microphone permission; documented in the README as a known weakness.
- Q: What SKR amounts should the four stake chips have? (OD-2) → A: Chips are SKR amounts tracking $10 / $25 / $50 / $100. The Operator checks the rate weekly and re-prices only when it has drifted drastically. Each cohort locks in the chip set current at its creation, so members of the same cohort who pick the same tier stake identical SKR. The contract validates SKR only and never reads a price. *(Revised in-session from daily re-pricing applied to all open cohorts. Revised 2026-10-06: re-priced every week, with no drift threshold — FR-056. Revised 2026-10-10: priced in the 4 hours before each weekly opening, so a new cohort locks a rate at most 4 hours old.)*
- Q: When does registration for a league's next cohort open, and can a member still in a running cohort join it? → A: Every league opens its next cohort the moment its current one starts; cohorts start weekly on Monday 05:00 UTC. Joining and leaving both close at a join cutoff **1 hour before the start**, after which the cohort is `Locked` until it starts. A member may hold one lobby place alongside a running cohort, independent of how that cohort ends. *(Revised in-session: briefly specified as join and leave open until the start with no `Locked` state, then rolled back.)*
- Q: Which extras beyond the core loop are in scope — daily streak, leaderboards, share cards, badges? → A: Only the daily streak (already specified) and leaderboards. Leaderboards rank on-chain progress only and exist to show discipline: per league, members are ranked by **survival rate** (cohorts cleared ÷ cohorts finished), among members with **at least 3 finished cohorts** in that league; cohorts played and league streak (consistency) are shown beside it. Each running cohort also has a roster. The daily streak stays personal and unranked. Share cards and badges are out of scope.
- Q: Who may trigger recording forfeits, settling a finished cohort and opening the next scheduled cohort — anyone, or only the Scheduler's key? → A: Anyone. The Scheduler is the usual caller, not a privileged one; the caller can never choose a parameter, outcome or amount. Publishing the chip set stays Operator-only.
- Q: What is the largest number of members a single cohort must support? → A: No limit is enforced; the expected size is up to 1,000 members per cohort, and the build is designed and tested for that.
- Q: When finisher payouts are rounded down to the smallest SKR unit, where do the leftover units go? → A: At settlement, the total rounding remainder is computed and sent to the fee destination, and recorded on the cohort.
- Q: At a day's deadline, should the survivor counter and projected prize change immediately for members who missed the day, or only once their forfeits are recorded? → A: Immediately. The survivor counter, projected pool, projected shares and cohort roster reflect every breach from the instant the deadline passes; claimable amounts come only from settlement.
- Q: How long may a paused session wait before it's abandoned, and how much time must be left before a session may start? → A: Neither — there is no single session. Verified reps are **banked in blocks of 10** on the device and kept for the whole day, so a member can do 10 + 10 + 5 across the day in separate sittings. Reps short of the next block when a sitting ends are discarded. Every sitting re-runs the environment checks and palm liveness. The app never refuses a late start; it shows reps still needed and time left, and warns clearly in the last 10 minutes.
- Q: Can someone do the free daily challenge and build a streak without connecting a wallet, or is a wallet required from the first screen? → A: A wallet is required from the first screen, before anything else — including the daily challenge, the streak, cohorts and leaderboards.
- Q: How does a member or judge with a freshly connected wallet get test SKR to stake and devnet SOL for transaction fees? → A: An in-app faucet: one tap gives a connected wallet enough test SKR for the top chip plus a little devnet SOL for fees, once per wallet per day, with no server involved. *(The once-per-wallet-per-day limit was removed 2026-10-12 — see Session 2026-10-12.)*
- Q: Are scripted members real on-chain wallets playing by the same contract rules, or display-only? → A: Real wallets, same rules. They stake test SKR, submit completed days and forfeit or finish on a planned schedule; the contract treats them like any member, so their forfeits fund real prizes and their finishes share them. They are labelled everywhere.
- Q: If a member finishes their last rep offline and the completed day can't reach the chain before the deadline, what happens? → A: Offline sittings are allowed and blocks still bank; the app warns clearly before an offline sitting and when a day completes offline, retries the submission automatically as soon as a connection returns, and the deadline stays strictly chain time — a day that lands late does not count. *(Superseded 2026-10-10 and 2026-10-11: nothing is sent while the app is closed. The day is sent only while Holdout is open and the chain answers, and the day-complete screen and the home say so — FR-024b, ES-3b.)*
- Q: When the palm check can't detect an open palm, how long does the app keep trying and what happens then? → A: After 30 seconds without a detected palm, show a tip and return to the environment checks; retries are unlimited, the member can cancel at any time, and banked reps are unaffected.
- Q: Given 13 days to the feature freeze, how should leaderboards, weekly chip re-pricing, delivery of a held carry-over and automatic offline retry be scheduled? → A: As **Phase 2 inside this spec**: built only after every Phase 1 item works end to end, with simple Phase 1 stand-ins. - Q: When can a member who just forfeited see the comeback, given that the program writes it on their league progress only when the forfeited Holdout settles, by which time joining has closed for every Holdout inside the 14-day window for most 21-day forfeits (open item L13)? - Q: Which Holdout is the comeback round, and for how long does it last? The command reads the chain's time again just before sending, since fetching the rate and the mint takes time: a run in those last 90 seconds, or one whose rate fetch ran into them, publishes nothing and reports that it is too late, and the cohorts opening at 05:00 copy the previous set (FR-057). An hourly run at `:17` never meets it. *(PR #30 review.)*
- Q: Since a transaction's blockhash lasts about 150 blocks rather than a fixed 90 seconds, how is a chip set kept from landing after 05:00 if block production slows, and what deadline does a set published by hand carry? (FR-055a, FR-056) → A: **The contract enforces it: every chip set is published with a deadline, and the contract refuses one that lands at or after it.** The weekly re-price signs the window's close, Monday 05:00 UTC, so a late set is refused instead of reaching only the next week's cohorts; the 90-second cutoff stays as the crank's own margin. A set published by hand (FR-055a) carries **chain time + 10 minutes** by default, and the Operator may give another deadline explicitly. The deadline is a time, never a price or a fiat figure (constitution III). *(PR #30 review.)*

Spec consistency, from the analysis of PR #41:

- Q: Should the sitting flow drop the `Liveness` state, so a sitting runs `Idle` → `Preflight` → `Countdown` → `Counting` with the held start position as the only gate before the countdown? (FR-020, US-3, §8.3) → A: **Yes.** The palm check was dropped on 2026-09-22 (FR-020, OD-3, T047) and nothing took its place as a liveness check: holding the start position is a readiness check. §8.3 loses the `Liveness` state and its rows, US-3 no longer promises that a live human is verified, and the Android contract's sitting row loses `Liveness` and the palm-timeout variant.
- Q: When an environment check fails, does the sitting end, or stay in pre-flight showing that check's fix, with a failure during the countdown going back to pre-flight? (FR-019, FR-020, §8.3) → A: **It never ends on a failed check.** In `Preflight` the failing check shows its fix and the sitting waits; the still-background check only advises (FR-019). During `Countdown`, a gating check failing, the camera view changing or the plank breaking returns to `Preflight` and the hold starts again (FR-020). Nothing banked changes either way; only the member cancelling or stopping, or the app being closed or crashing, leads back to `Idle`.
- Q: What moves a sitting from `Countdown` to `Counting`, and where does a sitting go when the app leaves the foreground mid-set? (FR-020, FR-024, §8.3) → A: **Counting begins when the spoken countdown ends with the checks still holding and the plank read; leaving the foreground starts over at the checks.** At "go" the counter must have read enough of the held plank to set the sitting's top and placement (002 FR-008); if it has not, the sitting goes back to `Preflight` and the hold starts again. When the app leaves the foreground during `Countdown` or `Counting`, the camera stops watching, so that sitting ends: reps short of the next block of 10 are discarded, banked blocks are kept, and the screen returns to `Preflight`, where a new sitting needs the checks and the held start position again. Only the member stopping, or the app being closed or crashing, returns to `Idle`. *(PR #41 review.)*
- Q: Does US-3's "refuses to start until every check passes" include the still-background check? (FR-019, US-3) → A: **No.** The lighting, whole-body and camera-view checks gate the start; the still-background check is shown with its fix and never blocks (FR-019, 2026-09-26). US-3 now says so. *(PR #41 review.)*
- Q: Does the countdown in §8.3 and AC-17 wait for "four checks green", or for every gating check? (FR-019, FR-020, §8.3, AC-17) → A: **Every gating check**, the still-background check only advising (FR-019, amended 2026-09-26). §8.3's `Preflight → Countdown` row and AC-17 say so without listing the checks, so they hold when a gating check is added or removed; FR-019 and US-3 name today's three.

## 5. User scenarios

### US-1 · First run

**Given** a person has installed the app and has a wallet
**When** they open it for the first time
**Then** they connect their wallet before anything else is available (FR-001a), are walked through the five-card tour (FR-067), land on Today (FR-069), and then see both levels open to join (Spark · 7-day and Habit · 21-day) — each open to them when their wallet holds a Seeker Genesis Token (FR-003, FR-003b) — and can start today's Daily 25 in one tap.

**And given** their wallet holds no Seeker Genesis Token
**Then** the Daily 25, the streak and every Holdout's roster are still theirs to use and watch; where they would join, the app says staked Holdouts are exclusive to Seeker owners and links to the Seeker store (FR-003c).

### US-2 · Joining from the lobby

**Given** a cohort is open for joining
**When** the member joins
**Then** their stake is taken and held separately from any prize pool, they appear in the roster immediately, and they see a countdown to the join cutoff and to the start, the live roster count, and an unconditional way to leave until joining closes.

**And given** they leave before the join cutoff
**Then** the full stake is returned with no deduction of any kind.

### US-3 · A day's reps

**Given** a member's cohort is running and today's reps are not yet done
**When** they start a sitting
**Then** the app checks the environment and refuses to start until the lighting, whole-body, knees-visible and camera-view checks pass, telling them exactly what to fix (the still-background check only advises, FR-019); waits until they hold the start position, a readiness check and not a liveness check (FR-020, OD-3); counts reps that meet the form rules and silently discards ones that do not; banks every block of 10 verified reps on the device; and the moment the banked total reaches the day's requirement, records the day as complete.

**And given** they stop part-way — say after 10, then come back for 10 more, then 5
**Then** each sitting starts with the checks and the held start position again, banked blocks carry across sittings for the whole day, and reps short of the next block of 10 when a sitting ends are not kept.

**And** their own day counter and the crowd strip on Today update on completion, and the survivor counter and the prize figure on Standing (FR-069a, FR-069b).

**And given** they allowed notifications when they joined
**Then** the phone reminds them when the Holdout starts and, on any day not yet recorded, before that day's deadline (and halfway through a day of 12 hours or more) — and stays quiet on a day already on chain; a day done on the phone but not yet on chain stays in the notification shade as a silent notice to submit it, re-posted silently every hour until it is on chain or its deadline passes (FR-068).

### US-4 · Missing a day

**Given** a member has not completed today's reps
**When** the day's deadline passes
**Then** their stake is forfeited, 30% is taken as fee and the remainder is added to that cohort's prize pool, their league progress resets, and they are told what happened and offered the next cohort of the same league as the way back, with today's Daily 25 as a separate secondary button while it is not done — with the exact amount that finishing the comeback round brings back (10 % of the lost stake, FR-037e; after a last-day miss, "Join today's round" late or the next one), when it starts, how long joining stays open and a Join straight into the stake sheet (FR-037g; amended 2026-10-11, 2026-10-12). Their daily streak is untouched, said as one small quiet line (FR-052).

**And** they cannot submit any further completed day for that cohort, whether or not the Scheduler has run yet.

### US-5a · The Daily 25

**Given** any member with a connected wallet, on any day — in a cohort or not, staking or not
**When** they open it
**Then** today's challenge is there: 25 reps, the same verification and the same banking in blocks of 10 across sittings as a cohort day, no stake, no prize, no deadline beyond the day itself. Completing it extends their streak.

**And** a member already in a cohort does not train twice — the cohort day is the day's challenge.

**And** nothing is ever paid against a streak.

### US-6 · Staking

**Given** a member whose wallet holds a Seeker Genesis Token is joining a cohort of either league (both are open in this build — FR-003, FR-003b)
**When** they choose their stake
**Then** they choose one of the four chips — labelled by its USD tier ($10 / $25 / $50 / $100) and showing the exact SKR amount locked for this cohort, with the USD value marked approximate — and see **their level's rule sentence** — the Spark's *"At 45° only. Aim for full push-ups from your toes; push-ups from the knees still count."* or the Habit's *"At 45° only: phone on the floor ahead of your shoulder. Full push-ups from your toes — push-ups from the knees don't count."* (FR-021a) — the daily obligation, the time until joining closes and until the start, the duration, and the cohort's current prize pool, and the plain statement that **if nobody drops out they get exactly their stake back**.

### US-7 · Watching the crowd thin

**Given** a member is in a running cohort
**When** other members forfeit
**Then** the survivor counter falls, the prize figure rises, and both are the largest things on the Holdout's detail under Standing (FR-038); Today carries them only as its one crowd strip, "{standing} of {total} still standing · your share {share} SKR" (FR-069a). The member can see how many days remain, their own completion record, and their projected share.

**And** both figures move at the day's deadline itself, for every member at once — not whenever a forfeit happens to be recorded (FR-038a).

### US-8 · Settlement and claim

**Given** a cohort has reached its end and the member completed every day
**When** settlement occurs
**Then** every finisher settles at the same moment on the same figures, and each is invited to claim their stake plus their proportional share of the pool. Claiming is a deliberate act the member performs, not an automatic deposit.

**And** while the cohort is settling, the member sees that automation settles it on its next pass, usually within 15 minutes of the end, as a countdown from the end (FR-035a); and a member who allowed notifications gets one notice 15 minutes after the end that their claim is probably ready (FR-068b).

### US-9 · A judge installs the app cold

**Given** a person installs the app on any day during judging (13 October – 9 November 2026) with no prior state
**When** they open it and connect a wallet
**Then** there is a real-time cohort running with members visibly thinning, today's Daily 25 is available to do immediately, and a Hackathon round is open to join, so the whole cycle — join, sittings, forfeit, settle, claim — can be run in about half an hour (FR-044a) with a wallet that holds a Seeker Genesis Token.

**And given** the judge's wallet holds no Seeker Genesis Token
**Then** they still see a running cohort thin, from the "Running now" list in Standing's empty state (FR-062a), and can do the Daily 25 at once (constitution XII); joining any Holdout tells them it is Seeker-exclusive and links to the Seeker store (FR-003c). The demo video shows the full cycle.

**And** the app, the README, the demo video and the pitch deck state plainly that the members of judging-period cohorts are scripted (constitution XI, FR-066).

### US-10 · Leaderboards *(league boards: Phase 2 · cohort roster: Phase 1)*

**Given** any member with a connected wallet, in a cohort or not
**When** they open a league's all-time leaderboard **[Phase 2]**
**Then** they see who is disciplined: members ranked by the share of their cohorts they survived in that league, each shown with that survival percentage, how many cohorts they have played, how many they cleared, and their current and best league streak — built entirely from on-chain progress.

**And** only members who have finished at least 3 cohorts in that league are ranked; everyone else is listed underneath as "not ranked yet", with their numbers visible and how many more cohorts they need. A member sees their own position or how far they are from being ranked.

**And given** a member is in a running cohort
**When** they open that cohort's roster
**Then** members still standing are listed first and forfeited members follow, ordered by how many days they lasted.

**And** no leaderboard shows or ranks any stake, prize or payout amount, and the daily streak appears on none of them.

## 6. Functional requirements

### Access

- **FR-001** A member is identified by their wallet.
- **FR-001a** Connecting a wallet is the first step in the app and is required before anything else is usable — the Daily 25, the streak, cohorts, rosters and leaderboards included. *(Amended 2026-10-09: the recorded walkthrough, once the one exception, is gone — FR-044.)* Before a wallet is connected, only the connect screen and, when the phone has no wallet app, help getting one are reachable. A person without a wallet is told plainly that one is needed and how to get one. The daily streak is kept on the device for the connected wallet.
- **FR-001b** *(added 2026-09-27)* **Devnet wallets only.** The app asks the wallet to authorize it on devnet and refuses to connect, or to have anything signed, when the wallet reports that it authorized another network — checked at connect and again inside every signing session, before a signature is requested. The member is told, in one sentence, that the wallet is not on devnet and nothing was signed, to switch it to devnet and connect again (*"Your wallet isn't on devnet, so nothing was signed. Switch it to devnet, then connect again (Me → Disconnect wallet)."*; the connect screen, where there is no Me yet, says the same without the parenthesis) *(wording 2026-10-11, evening)*, and the saved authorization is dropped so the next connect asks afresh. A wallet that reports no network (MWA 1.x) cannot be checked and is allowed.
- **FR-002** *(Amended 2026-10-10, twice.)* Each league is joinable by anyone whose wallet holds a Seeker Genesis Token (FR-003b) and who has a stake, whenever it has a cohort open, and it never closes to a member — after a clear or after a forfeit, in either league.
- **FR-003** *(Amended 2026-10-10.)* There is no ladder between the leagues. The 7-day and 21-day leagues are two separate products for different people, and both are open at all times to every member who may stake at all (FR-003b): neither requires a clear, a history or anything else earned in the other, and no gate, flag or qualification record for a ladder exists anywhere in the system. The Seeker requirement of FR-003b is the same for every staked Holdout and is not a ladder.
- **FR-003b** *(Added 2026-10-10.)* **Staked Holdouts are Seeker-exclusive.** Joining any staked Holdout — the 7-day, the 21-day and the short test Holdout (FR-044a) — requires the member's wallet to hold a Seeker Genesis Token: a Token-2022 token account owned by that wallet, with an amount of at least 1, whose mint is a member of the Seeker Genesis Token group — an account emptied by a transfer never counts. On mainnet the contract checks it when `genesis_gate_enabled` is on (research R17), and it is turned on at mainnet launch. On devnet, where Seeker Genesis Tokens do not exist, the flag stays off and the app checks the connected wallet's token on Solana mainnet (the same address) before it asks the wallet to sign a join; the automation and scripted members, which do not use the app, are not checked. The app's check can be turned off **only in a debug build**, with the Gradle property `-Pholdout.seekerGate=off` read into `BuildConfig.SEEKER_GATE`, so the emulator, a local validator and a tester's phone without a Seeker can reach the stake confirm (research R17, quickstart §5a); a release build always sets the flag on and always checks. That devnet enforcement lives in the app rather than the contract, and the README names it as a known weakness (constitution XI). Leaving a lobby place, submitting a day and claiming are never gated, so nothing already staked is ever stranded.
- **FR-003c** *(Added 2026-10-10.)* **A wallet without a Seeker Genesis Token can use everything except joining.** It does the Daily 25 and builds a streak, and it sees every running Holdout, its roster and its crowd thinning, from the "Running now" list in Standing's empty state (FR-062a). Where it would join, the app says plainly that staked Holdouts are exclusive to Seeker owners and links to the Seeker store, and offers no join.
- **FR-003d** *(Added 2026-10-10.)* **If the app cannot complete the check** — no network, a rate limit or a mainnet RPC outage — joining is refused with a message to try again ("We couldn't check your Seeker — try again"), never with the Seeker-exclusive notice; only a confirmed absence of the token shows that notice. A wallet once confirmed to hold the token is remembered on the phone, so a later outage never blocks it.
- **FR-003a** *(Removed 2026-10-10 — nothing is unlocked, so nothing is recorded; see FR-003.)*
- **FR-004** *(Removed 2026-10-10 — there is no unlock to keep; see FR-003.)*
- **FR-005** Daily 25 days completed outside a cohort grant no prize and no league progress — only the streak.
- **FR-006** A member may be in at most one running cohort at a time.
- **FR-006a** A member may additionally hold at most one lobby place (a `Pending` membership) at any time, including while in a running cohort, in either league. The lobby place is independent of the running cohort: finishing or forfeiting it changes nothing about the lobby place, which the member can still leave for a full refund until that cohort's join cutoff.
- **FR-006b** A lobby place is accepted only in a cohort that starts no earlier than the final deadline of the member's running cohort, so a member never carries two daily obligations at once. A 7-day member can hold a place in the cohort starting the instant theirs ends; a 21-day member cannot hold a place in a cohort starting while theirs still runs. *(Added 2026-10-12.)* The app never presents a Join the program would refuse (FR-006a, this rule): it lists and offers only the Holdouts the member can join now — none while they hold a lobby place, and while a Holdout runs only those that start at or after its end — with no greyed Join and no reason line (FR-053, FR-069c). To change their mind a member first leaves the lobby (FR-070), then joins another; there is no combined switch.

### Cohorts and the lobby

- **FR-007** A cohort has a fixed start, a fixed join cutoff, a fixed duration, a fixed daily rep requirement, a fixed day length, a fixed **exercise** and a fixed chip set, all set at creation and immutable thereafter. Only push-ups are accepted in this build; the parameter exists so a later exercise never has to change a running cohort or an existing account layout. *(2026-10-05, H35:)* a cohort also has a fixed **form**, copied from its league — 0, from the toes (internally `Hard`; members read **Habit · 21-day**), or 1, knees allowed (internally `Easy`; **Spark · 7-day**) — and the app refuses to start a sitting for a form it does not know.
- **FR-007a** Cohorts start weekly, on Monday at the 05:00 UTC boundary. Every league opens its next cohort for joining at the instant its current cohort starts, so each league has exactly one cohort open for joining at all times except the locked hour before each start. For the 21-day league this means up to three cohorts running at once. Opening is automated and needs no manual action (FR-043), and may be triggered by anyone once it is due (FR-030a).
- **FR-007b** The deployed contract remains upgradeable through the judging period, and an upgrade may only fix a defect. An upgrade never changes the rules, parameters or stored layout of any existing cohort, membership or progress record, and never moves a held stake or prize pool except through the rule-driven steps every member already relies on. The upgrade authority is named in the README as a known weakness, with its real fix — revoking it once the contract is final — beside it (constitution XI).
- **FR-008** A cohort accepts joins until its join cutoff, **1 hour before its start**, and starts for everyone at the same instant. *(Amended 2026-10-12: with one exception — the member whose comeback round it is may join it late, until its first day's deadline, FR-037e.)*
- **FR-009** Until the join cutoff, a member may leave and receive a full refund, unconditionally.
- **FR-010** From the join cutoff there is no way out and no refund; the roster is final and the daily obligation begins at the start. Whether a join or a leave landed before the cutoff is decided by chain time alone, never by when the member signed or by whether any automated process has run. *(Amended 2026-10-12: the roster is final from the join cutoff for everyone except the comeback late join of FR-037e, which can add a member to a `Locked` or `Running` cohort until its first day's deadline.)*
- **FR-011** Every cohort is staked. A cohort holds each member's stake separately from the prize pool at all times.
- **FR-011a** No maximum cohort size is enforced. The expected size is **up to 1,000 members**: recording forfeitures and settlement work in pages so that no single step depends on cohort size, and rosters and leaderboards remain correct at that size. The build is tested at 1,000 members; larger cohorts must still be correct, only untested.
- **FR-054** There are four stake chips, identified by USD tier: $10, $25, $50 and $100. The same tiers apply to both leagues.
- **FR-055** Every cohort copies the chip set current at its creation and keeps it for its whole life (FR-007). The contract accepts a join only for exactly one of that cohort's four SKR amounts, validates SKR only, and never reads a price.
- **FR-055a** *(Phase 1)* At launch the Operator publishes **one chip set**, converting each USD tier to SKR at that day's off-chain rate. Every cohort created in Phase 1 locks that set. Every chip set is published with a **deadline**, and the contract refuses one that lands at or after it; a set published by hand carries chain time + 10 minutes unless the Operator gives another.
- **FR-055b** No cohort can be created before a chip set has been published. A cohort keeps the amounts it copied for its whole life (FR-007), so one opened in the gap between deployment and the first publish would hold four amounts of zero and take zero-stake joins until it settled, and nothing could correct it. Opening a cohort is permissionless (FR-030a), so this is enforced by the contract and not by the order the Operator happens to run things in.
- **FR-056** **[Phase 2]** Once a week, in the **publish window** — the 4 hours before each weekly boundary, Monday 01:00 UTC up to, not including, Monday 05:00 UTC, when the next cohorts open (FR-007a) — the Operator **re-prices the chip set** from the off-chain rate: each USD tier is converted to SKR at that rate and rounded to a readable SKR amount, and the new set is published — every week, with no drift threshold, even when the amounts come out the same. The cohort opening at 05:00 copies it, so its chips are priced at most 4 hours before it opens and stay at that rate for every member who joins it. The re-pricing is the Operator's own scheduled command, never the always-on automation, run **every hour** in whatever time zone the Operator's machine keeps, and it publishes **at most once per publish window**, by chain time: a run outside the window does nothing, no send starts in the window's last 90 seconds — checked again by chain time just before sending — and the set's deadline (FR-055a) is the window's close, so the contract refuses one that would land after the boundary, the first run inside it publishes, and a run that fails or finds no rate (FR-057) is retried by the next hourly run inside the same window. A new set applies only to cohorts created after it; it never changes a cohort that already exists.
- **FR-057** **[Phase 2]** If no rate is available at any run in the publish window, nothing is published that week: the current chip set stays in force and the cohort opening at the boundary copies it. Opening cohorts and joining never depend on a price being available.
- **FR-058** A member's stake is the SKR amount taken at join and never changes afterwards, whatever the rate does. Within one cohort, every member who chose the same tier holds an identical SKR stake; payouts remain proportional to stake (FR-032).
- **FR-059** Every figure the member will actually stake or receive is shown in SKR. USD tier labels and USD equivalents are marked approximate and never presented as what the member will receive (FR-039). *(Amended 2026-10-11.)* The only dollar figure the app shows is the "≈ $" beside a stake tier chip, with "Dollar values are approximate." near it: no payout, prize pool, total, share or amount at stake carries one, and there is no carry-over line.

### The daily obligation

- **FR-013** Each day of the cohort, the day's required reps must be verified before that day's deadline — in one sitting or several (FR-024).
- **FR-014** The rep requirement **and the exercise** are read from the cohort, never from a constant.
- **FR-015** Unlimited retries within the same day.
- **FR-016** No grace day, no postponement, no carry-forward. One missed day ends the cohort for that member.

### Session verification

The fourth check asks **whether the camera can see enough of the member to measure them**, not whether it is placed to one side: the phone may stand in front of them or beside them, the app names which it is, and only a view it cannot classify at all is refused. ### Forfeiture

- **FR-026** On breach, the member's entire stake is forfeited. No partial credit.
- **FR-027** *(Amended 2026-10-05, H34, H35; re-split 2026-10-09; re-split 2026-10-12.)* A forfeited stake is split by fixed program rule: **70%** to that cohort's prize pool, **10%** pledged to the forfeiting member's own comeback (FR-037c, FR-037e), **10%** to the developer destination and **10%** to the operations destination *(2026-10-12; was 5% to the league's boost reserve and 5% to its comeback reserve — no share goes straight to a boost any more)*. The fee is `floor(30% × stake)` and the prize pool takes the remainder, so the pool rounds up, not down; within the fee the pledge and developer parts are rounded down and the operations part takes whatever the rounding leaves, so the parts always add up to the stake *(clarified 2026-10-11, updated 2026-10-12: stake 101 gives pool 71, comeback 10, developer 10, operations 10)*. The pledge stays in the cohort's vault until settlement counts the forfeit (FR-037c).
- **FR-028** Forfeiture resets the member's league streak in that league and ends their participation in that cohort. The reset is written when that cohort settles, in cohort order, so it can never be undone by an earlier cohort settling later (FR-061a).
- **FR-030** The automated process that records forfeitures is safe to run twice, and safe to run late.
- **FR-030a** Recording forfeitures, settling a finished cohort, and opening a league's next cohort are **permissionless**: any wallet may trigger them once they are due. The caller cannot choose or influence any parameter, outcome, recipient or amount — each step only does what on-chain state and the schedule already require, and does nothing if it is not yet due or already done. A step that is not yet due may refuse plainly instead of silently doing nothing — settling a cohort before the previous cohort of its league has settled is refused (FR-061a) — and the caller simply runs it again once it is due. Publishing a chip set is the only Operator-restricted instruction; the contract's upgrade authority is governed by FR-007b.

### Settlement and claim

- **FR-031** Every member of a cohort settles on the same figures at the same moment.
- **FR-032** A finisher's payout is their stake plus `pool × (their stake ÷ total stake of all finishers)`.
- **FR-032a** Each finisher's share is rounded **down** to the smallest SKR unit. At settlement, the total rounding remainder — the pool minus the sum of all rounded-down shares, always fewer units than there are finishers — is sent to the fee destination and recorded on the cohort. After settlement the pool holds exactly the finishers' shares.
- **FR-033** The payout denominator is computed once and never recomputed.
- **FR-034** Payout is claimed by a deliberate member action, never deposited automatically.
- **FR-035** An unclaimed payout remains claimable indefinitely.
- **FR-035a** *(Added 2026-10-11.)* **When a finished Holdout settles.** Between the end and settlement, the cohort detail (H4) and the Home/H0 card tell a member who finished that automation settles it on its next pass, **usually within 15 minutes of the end** — automation passes every 10 minutes (R12) and settles a finished cohort in one pass — shown as time since the end and time left of those 15 minutes, never a clock time (constitution VI). Past them the wording says it is taking longer than usual and settles on the next pass, and that nothing the member is owed can change. It is never a promise (constitution XIII): settlement is permissionless (FR-030a) and waits for the league's earlier cohort (FR-061a). On Today, after the last day is on chain, the line "That was the last day. It settles soon after the end, then your claim is ready." stands alone: the settling card is on Standing's detail, where the app moves at the end (FR-069a; amended 2026-10-12).
- **FR-037** If a cohort settles with no finishers, its whole prize pool — the 70% parts of its forfeits — goes to its league's boost reserve, recorded on the settled cohort as `no_finisher_boost`. The developer and operations already took their 10% + 10% when each forfeit was recorded, and the pledges of its forfeiters stay theirs (FR-037c). Nothing carries over into another cohort's pool directly (the reserve feeds later boosts only through FR-037d's cap), so settling it needs no other cohort's money. *(Amended 2026-10-05, H34, H35; half each to the developer and operations 2026-10-09; to the league's boost reserve 2026-10-12.)*
- **FR-037a** *(Superseded 2026-10-05 by FR-037: there is no carry-over, so no destination to choose, nothing held and nothing to deliver.)*
- **FR-037b** *(Superseded 2026-10-05: no cohort receives a carry-over.)*
- **FR-037c** *(Added 2026-10-05, H35; rewritten 2026-10-12.)* **The pledge is the member's, and it is set aside when the forfeit is counted.** When settlement counts a member's forfeit (`settle_count`), their 10% pledge moves from the cohort's vault into the league's program-owned reserve vault and is booked to the league's `comeback_reserve`, which means *the sum of open pledges plus refunds owed but not yet drawn* — money no boost may touch. At the same moment the member's league progress opens the comeback (FR-037e). Until a forfeit is counted its pledge waits in the cohort's vault (`comeback_due`). Counting runs in cohort order within a league (FR-061a), so the reserve a cohort can draw on depends only on earlier cohorts of its league, never on when automation recorded anyone's forfeit (constitution II). A pledge that is replaced or released (FR-037e, FR-037h) leaves `comeback_reserve` and joins the league's `boost_reserve`; no token moves, because both live in the one reserve vault. The boost reserve receives nothing else but the pool of a cohort nobody finished (FR-037).
- **FR-037d** *(Added 2026-10-05, H35; cap 10% since 2026-10-10, was 20%; fed differently since 2026-10-12.)* **Prize boost.** When a cohort with at least one finisher finalizes, its prize pool first receives `min(10% of its own pool, the league's boost reserve)` from that reserve; the shares are computed on the boosted pool, and the boost is recorded on the cohort. A cohort with no forfeits receives no boost. The boost draws **only** from the boost reserve, never from the comeback reserve, so a member's comeback is never spent by a boost; the boost reserve is fed by comebacks nobody made good (released pledges, FR-037e, FR-037h) and by the pools of cohorts nobody finished (FR-037). The boost is drawn at finalization, before the shares.
- **FR-037e** *(Added 2026-10-05, H35; refund raised to 20% 2026-10-09; rewritten 2026-10-12: a guaranteed 10% pledge, the next round, a late join after a last-day miss.)* **Comeback refund.** When settlement counts a member's forfeit, their league progress opens the comeback: it keeps the forfeited stake (`comeback_stake`), the league (`comeback_league`) and the rounds in which it can be made good, from `comeback_round` (**first**) to `comeback_last_round` (**last**) — only the latest counted forfeit, a new one replacing the old pledge (below). **The comeback rounds** are cohorts of the same league (matched by `league_id`, since every test league shares one progress slot). `first` is the league's first cohort that starts after the missed deadline: the smallest index k with `first_start + k × cadence > missed_deadline`. Usually that round is still open to join and it is the only one (`last = first`). When it is no longer open to join the second after the miss (`missed_deadline + 1 ≥` its join cutoff — exactly when the miss is on a round's last day, since that deadline is the second before the next round starts) the member **chooses**: join `first` **late** — the member whose comeback round it is may join it until its first day's deadline, even after joining has closed for everyone else (FR-008, FR-010) — or join `first + 1`, open as usual, and then `last = first + 1`. Examples: a 7-day league, a miss on Wednesday → next Monday's round only; a miss on Sunday (day 7) → this Monday's round (late, until the end of its day 1) or next Monday's. A 21-day league, a miss on a Sunday (day 7 or 14) → the round starting Monday (late) or the next one; a miss on Wednesday → the next round open to join. The pledge is valid for those rounds only, not for a number of days. **Made good:** when the member is counted as a finisher of any cohort from `first` to `last` of that league (the first one finished uses it), they are owed `min(10% of the forfeited stake, their new stake)`; any excess of the pledge over that is released to the boost reserve at once; the owed part is drawn from the comeback reserve at that cohort's finalization, in full — it is always there — and paid with their claim. There is no pro-rata cut and no "while the reserve lasts". **Released to the boost** (the pledge leaves `comeback_reserve` and joins `boost_reserve`; no token moves): when the member is counted again in that league while the old pledge is still open — a forfeit (its new pledge replaces the old one) or a finish of a cohort after `last` — and by `release_comeback` (FR-037h). A finish of a cohort before `first` changes nothing. **The late join:** `join_cohort` takes two optional accounts, `comeback_membership` (the member's forfeited or in-breach membership) and `comeback_cohort` (its cohort), from which the program works out the missed deadline and `first`; with them the member joins `first` while it is `Locked` or `Running`, until its first day's deadline, and a late join into a running cohort takes the running slot at once; without them, or outside that window, joining a closed cohort is still `JoiningClosed` (6021). *Test builds only:* a forfeit counted in league B while the shared test slot holds an open pledge of league A does not open a comeback; its 10% goes to B's boost reserve, and A's pledge waits for `release_comeback` on A. The app offers the comeback from the forfeit itself, not from this record (FR-037g).

- **FR-037h** *(Added 2026-10-12; rule refined the same day.)* **Releasing a comeback nobody made good.** `release_comeback` is **permissionless** (FR-030a): any wallet may call it, and the crank does. ## 8. Behaviour: states and transitions

### 8.1 Cohort

`Open` → `Locked` → `Running` → `Settling` → `Settled`

| From → To | Trigger | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `[*] → Open` | the league's current cohort starts (FR-007a) | joining and leaving open; chip set and all parameters fixed |
| `Open → Locked` | join cutoff, 1 hour before start | joining and leaving both close; the roster is final |
| `Locked → Running` | start instant | every lobby membership becomes active |
| `Running → Settling` | final deadline | no further completed days accepted |
| `Settling → Settled` | settlement completes | finisher count and denominator written once |

`Settling` exists so that settlement can be resumed after an interruption without double-crediting anyone, and so no completed day can land against a day already scored.

`Locked` exists so that "joining has closed but the cohort has not started" is a legal, visible state: the roster and member count are final for the last hour, and no join or leave can land in the same instant the obligation begins. All time-triggered transitions are derived from chain time and take effect whether or not any automated process has run.

### 8.2 Membership

`Pending` → `Active` → one of `Forfeited` / `ReadyToClaim` → `Claimed`, with `Withdrawn` reachable from `Pending`

| From → To | Trigger | Guard |
|---|---|---|
| `[*] → Pending` | member joins | cohort `Open` (before its join cutoff); member holds no other `Pending` membership; cohort starts no earlier than the final deadline of any running membership (FR-006a, FR-006b) |
| `Pending → Withdrawn` | member leaves | cohort `Open` (before its join cutoff) |
| `Pending → Active` | start instant | — |
| `Active → Forfeited` | deadline passes with the day incomplete | — |
| `Active → ReadyToClaim` | settlement | every day completed |
| `ReadyToClaim → Claimed` | member claims | — |

Leaving the lobby is a transition out of `Pending`, not out of `Active`. A `Pending` membership is unaffected by any transition of the same member's running membership.

### 8.3 Sitting and day progress

A sitting: `Idle` → `Preflight` → `Countdown` → `Counting`, then either back to `Idle` (sitting ended) or on to `Completed` → `Submitted`.

| From → To | Trigger | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| `Idle → Preflight` | member starts a sitting | shows reps still needed and time left; warns in the last 10 minutes (FR-047) |
| within `Preflight` | a gating check fails | that check shows what to fix and the sitting waits; the still-background check only advises (FR-019); nothing banked changes |
| `Preflight → Countdown` | every gating check green (lighting, whole body, knees, camera view; the still-background check only advises, FR-019) **and** the start position held about a second | no tap; spoken countdown; leaving the position restarts the hold and nothing banked changes (FR-020) |
| `Countdown → Counting` | the spoken countdown ends with the checks still holding and enough of the plank read to set the sitting's top and placement (002 FR-008) | counting begins; a descent begun on "1" counts |
| `Countdown → Preflight` | a gating check fails, the camera view changes or the plank breaks before "go", or too little of the plank was read by "go" | back to the checks; the hold starts again; nothing banked changes (FR-020) |
| `Preflight` or `Countdown → Idle` | member cancels | nothing banked changes |
| within `Counting` | every 10th verified rep of the day | a block of 10 is banked on the device |
| `Countdown` or `Counting → Preflight` | the app leaves the foreground | that sitting ends: reps short of the next block of 10 are discarded, banked blocks kept; the next sitting starts at the checks (FR-024) |
| `Counting → Idle` | sitting ends — member stops, the app is closed or crashes | reps short of the next block of 10 are discarded; banked blocks kept for the day |
| `Counting → Completed` | banked + current verified reps reach the day's requirement | the day is done |
| `Completed → Submitted` | the completed cohort day is confirmed on-chain | day counter advances; a Daily 25 day is recorded on the device instead |

Sittings may repeat without limit until the day's deadline, at which point that day's banked progress stops counting.

### 8.4 Invariants

- **INV-1** A cohort's vault always holds exactly its held stakes, plus its prize pool, plus the pledges of recorded forfeits not yet counted, plus comeback refunds drawn and not yet claimed *(amended 2026-10-12)*. A league's reserve vault always holds exactly its boost reserve plus its comeback reserve.
- **INV-2** Only forfeited stakes ever enter a prize pool: the cohort's own, or a boost drawn from its league's reserve, which holds nothing but parts of earlier forfeits of that league (released pledges and the pools of cohorts nobody finished).
- **INV-3** A member's completed days never exceed the days their cohort has reached, counting the day in progress: at the cohort's first second at most one day can be complete, and never more than the cohort's duration.
- **INV-4** Per member: at most one membership in a `Running` cohort, at most one `Pending` membership, and no `Pending` membership whose cohort starts before the final deadline of the member's running one — so no two daily obligations ever overlap.
- **INV-5** The payout denominator is written exactly once.
- **INV-6** After a cohort settles, the only change possible anywhere in it is a claim.
- **INV-7** Recording forfeitures and settling are both safe to repeat.
- **INV-8** A member in breach cannot submit, regardless of automation state.
- **INV-9** A member's league streak depends only on the order of their own cohorts, never on the order in which forfeits and settlements are processed.
- **INV-10** *(Added 2026-10-05; amended 2026-10-12.)* The boost reserve grows only at finalization (the pool of a cohort nobody finished), at counting (a pledge replaced, or the excess of a pledge over a smaller refund) and by `release_comeback`; it shrinks only by a boost drawn at finalization. `comeback_reserve` always covers every open pledge and every owed refund, and a boost can never draw from it. What a boost draws may depend on when the crank released an expired pledge; a pledge never does.

## 9. Open decisions

All resolved as of 2026-09-15. Kept here as the decision record.

### OD-1 · How a day's boundary is defined — **RESOLVED 2026-09-15**

**Day N runs from `cohort_start + N × day_length` for every member alike.** There is no timezone anywhere in the system; the contract derives every deadline from chain time and asks nothing of any client.

The boundary instant is **05:00 UTC** — chosen for Europe and the Americas together: Warsaw 07:00 and Kyiv 08:00, both before the common morning session; New York 01:00, dead of night; Los Angeles 22:00, which loses the last two evening hours and is the deliberate cost. Europe and US West have no mutually quiet hour — they are ten hours apart — so the only real choice was which side pays, and a boundary landing mid-morning in Kyiv would have been worse than one landing at 22:00 in Los Angeles.

The boundary instant is a **property of the cohort**, so regional cohorts later are configuration, not redesign.

Per-member local midnight, and the bounded-oracle pattern that would make it safe, are recorded in research R7 for a global launch. Evaluating deadlines in a backend was considered and rejected there.

### OD-2 · Stake chip amounts in SKR — **RESOLVED 2026-09-15: SKR chips tracking USD tiers, checked weekly, locked per cohort**

Four chips tracking $10 / $25 / $50 / $100. The contract accepts and validates SKR amounts only and never reads a price (constitution III). Once a week, in the 4 hours before the next cohorts open, the Operator re-prices the chips from the off-chain rate and publishes the new set; if no rate is available, the current set stays. Each cohort locks in the chip set current at its creation, so two members of one cohort on the same tier always hold the same SKR stake. See FR-054…FR-059, ES-10, ES-11. **Phasing (§3a):** Phase 1 publishes one chip set at launch (FR-055a); the weekly check and re-pricing are Phase 2.

The rate source and the Operator's re-pricing are trusted off-chain inputs and are named as such in the README (constitution XI).

### OD-3 · Audio in rep verification — **RESOLVED 2026-09-15: dropped, both**

The audio environment check and the audio rep condition are both removed. The environment check has five pills — four that gate the start (lighting, whole body, knees visible, camera view) and the still-background check, which only advises *(five since 2026-10-10; it was four items when this was answered)* — rep rules are visual only, and the app never asks for the microphone (FR-019, FR-021, FR-022a). The lost anti-spoofing filter is accepted for a devnet build with a test token and is documented in the README.

**Amended 2026-09-22, after the first sitting on real hardware: palm liveness is dropped too.** The palm asked someone already face-down on the floor to lift a hand off it — the same mistake as the "I'm ready" button it followed, and found the same way, by holding the phone. The start position replaces it (FR-020): the top of a push-up, which the pose model already sees, held for about a second.

This leaves the build with **no liveness check at all**. Nothing confirms a live person rather than a photograph, and nothing confirms the member who banks the first ten reps is the one who set the phone up. That is accepted on devnet with a test token and named in the README.

**The real fix is the one this decision already chose:** audio. Reinstating the audio check and the audio rep condition together restores liveness and anti-spoofing in one move, and it is a later phase of development, not a hackathon task. *Sound the app plays* is unaffected by any of this and is used freely — the countdown and the banked-block chimes are spoken and played aloud, because a member on the floor cannot read the screen. What is forbidden is the microphone, and `RECORD_AUDIO` remains undeclared.

### OD-4 · How a judge experiences a full cycle (FR-044) — **RESOLVED 2026-09-15: recorded walkthrough; revised 2026-10-09: the demo video, sent as a link**

Real-time cohorts run continuously through judging and until winners are announced (FR-043), populated by disclosed scripted members (FR-043). The full cycle is shown in the demo video, sent as a link with the submission, recorded from the real app on a cohort of the disclosed short test lobby — its settlement and a real claim (FR-044, FR-044a, amended 2026-10-09). The app carries no walkthrough. The judged 7-day and 21-day leagues run on real 24-hour days; the short test lobby exists on devnet only, keeps opening cohorts for any wallet holding a Seeker Genesis Token through the live period, and is labelled wherever it appears.

### OD-6 · One clock or two — **RESOLVED 2026-09-15: one clock**

The streak day resets on the same instant as the cohort day. There is exactly one notion of "today" in the app, for money and for habit alike.

Local-midnight streaks would have been safe — the streak is cosmetic and off-chain, so none of the reasons timezones were removed from the contract apply to it — but two notions of "today" in one app is a support burden that outweighs the gain. Revisit at launch, when the habit tier becomes the growth surface.

### OD-5 · What happens to an unclaimed pool when nobody finishes (FR-037) — **RESOLVED 2026-09-15: split 50/50; REVISED 2026-10-05: all to the fee destination; REVISED 2026-10-09: half to the developer, the rest to operations; REVISED 2026-10-12: to the league's boost reserve**

At settlement, half goes to the fee destination and half carries over into the prize pool of the next cohort of the same league that is still open for joining. If none is open, settlement waits: it is refused until someone opens that cohort, which anyone may do (revised 2026-09-19 — holding the carry-over on the settled cohort let a caller's timing decide whether it ever reached a prize pool). See FR-037…FR-037b, ES-7…ES-7b.

**Revised 2026-10-05:** the whole pool goes to the fee destination and nothing carries over, so settlement never waits on another cohort (FR-037).

**Revised 2026-10-09:** the fee now has two destinations, so the whole pool goes out half (rounded down) to the developer destination and the rest to the operations destination; still nothing carries over (FR-027, FR-037, FR-037f).

**Revised 2026-10-12:** the developer and operations already took their 10% + 10% of every forfeit when it was recorded, so the whole prize pool (the 70% parts) goes to the league's boost reserve, recorded as `no_finisher_boost`; nothing else is paid out and no member's pledge is touched (FR-037, FR-037c).

## 10. Acceptance criteria

The build is done when all of the following are demonstrable on devnet:

1. A member joins a 7-day cohort, completes 7 days of verified sessions and claims a payout. A member with no 7-day history joins a 21-day cohort.
2. A member joins a staked cohort, misses a day, and their stake appears in the pool minus exactly 30%.
3. A cohort settles once, and two finishers with equal stakes receive byte-identical payouts. In a cohort whose shares do not divide exactly, every share is rounded down, the remainder reaches the fee destination at settlement, and the sum of all claims equals total finisher stake plus pool minus that remainder, to the unit.
4. A finisher claims and the funds arrive.
5. Killing the automated process for a full day changes no outcome: on restart the same members are forfeited, the same payouts result, and no member in breach was able to submit in the interim. With the Scheduler still stopped, an unrelated wallet can record the forfeits, settle the cohort and open the next one, producing exactly the same state; the same wallet calling any of them early, twice, or with different arguments changes nothing.
6. The published settlement vectors all pass as tests.
7. All invariants INV-1…INV-9 hold under a randomised sequence of joins, sessions, misses and claims, including a cohort of 1,000 members whose forfeitures and settlement are processed across multiple pages — with pages run out of order and repeated — and whose roster entries (and, in Phase 2, leaderboard entries) match on-chain state.
