# Camera sitting

The sitting screen while counting: the member's own camera, full-bleed, with the count over it.

**Anatomy:** a full-bleed camera preview on `canvas`. A `scrim` band at the top holds banked reps (`label`) and time left (`countdown`). A `scrim` band at the bottom holds the live rep count in `counter`, with the coach cue or refused-rep hint as its caption. The pose skeleton is drawn in `accent` at 80%.

**Sequence:** pre-flight (four pills) → hold the start position → 3-second countdown → counting → block banked (*"20 banked"*) → day complete.

**Rules:** text over the camera always sits on a scrim. Each counted rep plays a short tone and a rising "+1", and every tenth plays the banked chime instead. Hints are shown large and spoken: *Go lower · Keep your body straight · Slow down*. In the last 10 minutes, the countdown turns `warning` but never blocks the sitting.
