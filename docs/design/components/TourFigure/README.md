# Tour figure

The side-view push-up figure on "How it works", card 3: it shows how to get into position, hold, and do the reps.

**Source of truth:** the Holdout Rig artifact (https://claude.ai/artifact/4JZcyBgWLbSA9CiZqd5VAZ). The tour card is generated from it, frame by frame; do not redraw the figure by hand.

**Canvas:** 400 × 240, side view, facing left, floor at y = 200, camera on a stool at the left. The figure sits inside a dashed outline that turns `success` when the whole body is in frame.

**Body:** Athletic preset. Skin `#568AA4`, a figure colour of its own, not a token and not `accent`. Shade, deep shade, highlight, outline and far side are derived from it (26 %, 58 % darker; 72 % lighter; 92 % darker for the outline; 16 % and 38 % darker for the far limbs).

**Shorts:** loose, waist to mid-thigh, following the belly and the glute, a flared leg and a curved hem. Cloth `#596166`, darker side `#3E4549`, the word HOLDOUT in white along the thigh. No fold lines, no waistband line, no stripe.

**Arm band:** on the forearm, just above the wrist. Its edges follow the arm and its ends are cut straight across it, so it wraps the arm instead of sitting on it like a capsule. Two thin strap lines.

**Motion (12 s loop):** stand with the phone 0.8 s → kneel 1.5 s → get into the top 1.2 s → four checks tick green 1.6 s → hold countdown 3, 2, 1 in 1.5 s → checks clear 0.4 s → three reps, 1.4 s each → rest at the top 0.8 s. The animation runs the countdown at half speed; the caption still says the hold is 3 s, because that is the rule.

**Rules:** the UI around the figure (camera cone, outline, check pills, countdown digits, rep count) keeps `accent` and `success`; only the body uses the figure colour. With reduced motion, show the top pose and the finished checks, nothing moving.
