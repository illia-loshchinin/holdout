# Launch: store, web, social and deck

How the brand shows up outside the app for the devnet launch and the hackathon submission. Everything here follows the brand book: the naming table, the words to use and never use, and the disclosures that are always stated.

## The one-line story

Use these, in this order, wherever the brand introduces itself:

- Tagline: ***Be the holdout. Stake on yourself.***
- What it is: *Holdout is the push-up challenge you stake on. Do 25 push-ups a day, counted by your phone's camera. Miss a day and your stake goes into the prize pool. Finish and you get your stake back plus a share of the pool.*
- The free way in: *The Daily 25 is free, every day. A Holdout is 7 or 21 days with a stake.*

## Store listing

- **Title and subtitle:** *Holdout — the push-up challenge you stake on*
- **Icon:** `holdout-icon-store-512.png` from the App icon group. The store applies its own mask.
- **Banner and feature images:** the horizontal logo centred on `canvas`, nothing else, or one real screen beside it. No gradient: the Solana gradient belongs to day complete and claim only.
- **Screenshots:** real screens from the build you submit, never mockups that differ from it. In this order: Today (the Daily 25), joining a Holdout (the stake sheet), the sitting (the count over the camera), Standing (the survivor counter), Claim. One short caption above each, in sentence case: *Do your reps* · *Stake on yourself* · *Your phone counts every push-up* · *Watch the crowd thin* · *Finish and claim your share*.
- **Description:** start with the one-line story, then the Daily 25, then how a Holdout works with the fee sentence below, then the disclosures. Check the store's current size and length limits before export.

## Disclosures, everywhere money shows

These come from the brand book and go on the listing, the website and the deck as well as in the app:

- *Test network, test token.*
- *If nobody drops out, you get exactly your stake back.*
- *Stakes and payouts are in SKR; dollar values are approximate.*
- *Some members in these Holdouts are scripted.*
- The split, in one sentence: *Miss one and you lose your stake. 80 % of it stays with members: 70 % to the prize pool and 10 % set aside for your comeback. 20 % goes to the project. If nobody finishes, the pool boosts later Spark · 7-day prizes.* (name the level and length of the Holdout in question)

Never: *bet, wager, gamble, odds, luck, jackpot, pot, winnings, returns, yield, earnings*, or a dollar figure presented as what someone will receive.

## Website and social

- Hook with *challenge*, name with *Holdout*: *"Take the 7-day push-up challenge. Join a Holdout, stake on yourself, finish and share the pool with everyone still standing."*
- Black page, white type, the horizontal logo top left. One `accent` call to action. Big numbers carry the hero, as in the app: a survivor counter such as `189 / 312` beats a stock photo.
- Social avatar: the mark alone on black (stacked lockup without the name). Header images: the crowd on `canvas` with the tagline.
- Imagery: real screens and real people doing push-ups filmed on the phone. No stock fitness photos, no coins, no rockets, no emoji.

## Pitch deck and demo video

- Title slide: stacked logo on black, the tagline under it, nothing else.
- Use *Holdout* throughout and explain *cohort* once on the mechanics slide: *"everyone in a Holdout starts and finishes together"*.
- The video shows the build judges will install, filmed on a Seeker, with the test-network disclosure visible on every staking screen.
- Fonts in slides: Roboto Flex for big numbers, Roboto for text, JetBrains Mono for SKR amounts and addresses.
